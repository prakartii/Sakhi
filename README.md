# Sakhi: Empowering Indian Women

These 8 screenshots are my product Sakhi (by Yuukke Catalyst) — an AI business platform for Indian women entrepreneurs. Keep the feature set, nav, copy, "Why:" lines, ₹ figures, and mic-first structure exactly as-is. I want two changes: a lighter palette, and a more distinctive, less "AI-generated-template" feel.

1. Palette — lighter and warmer. Shift to a lighter, airier beige/ivory base (soft cream, warm off-white, pale sand). Keep the deep accent (used sparingly for primary buttons + active nav). Overall it should feel light, warm, and premium — not saturated, not dark.

2. Make it distinctive, not templated. Right now it reads as a generic AI-generated site — evenly-sized cards, default spacing, no personality. Add craft and interactivity that reflect Indian women entrepreneurs and handloom/craft culture:

Subtle motion: gentle fade/slide-in as sections enter the viewport; soft hover states on cards (lift + shadow, or a reveal of the "Why:" detail on hover); a living waveform on the mic.

Distinctive components instead of uniform boxes: vary card sizes and layouts (a hero feature card larger than its neighbours, an asymmetric bento-style grid rather than an even matrix), so no screen is just a tidy grid of equal boxes.

Craft-inspired visual texture: warm handloom/textile-weave motifs, block-print pattern accents, or thread/stitch dividers used sparingly as section separators or backgrounds — enough to feel handcrafted and Indian, not sterile. Keep the handwritten note accents.

Warm micro-details: soft rounded organic shapes, a subtle grain/paper texture on the background, gentle depth — so it feels designed by a person, not generated.

Keep it AI-business, not decorative: it must still read as a serious business platform — the texture and motion are accents, never clutter. Every card keeps its "Why:" and "Based on what you've shared" lines and ₹ figures. Don't sacrifice the professional, data-driven feel for prettiness; layer personality on top of it.

Do not change: features, nav sections, wording, or the mic-first structure. This is a visual identity + interactivity pass only.
